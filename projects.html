$ cd orion-2wd/

Orion 2WD

An experimental two-wheeled robot for learning robotics and embedded systems. It runs on a NodeMCU ESP8266 with a TB6612 motor driver and N20 motors, is controlled over WiFi through a web interface, and has an SPI TFT display (ST7789) plus QTR-8RC sensor tests. Line following, obstacle detection and computer vision are on the roadmap.

  • ESP8266
  • Embedded
  • Robotics
